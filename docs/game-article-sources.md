## Editorial scope

Historical photos are explicitly labeled. The Perfect World building is the publisher’s Beijing building, not a verified Hotta development office. Kuro’s reception photo is not labeled as its development team. No generated people or buildings are used.

The article date is an editorial review date, not a fabricated historical publication date. Development start dates, public announcements, and regional release dates are distinguished. Announced acquisition agreements are not labeled as completed transactions.

## Search configuration

Set `NUXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the production origin (for example, your actual HTTPS domain) when deploying. When unset, canonical links, structured data, robots.txt and sitemap.xml use the request origin. The sitemap lists the existing public pages and all six article routes, with article image URLs.

Article metadata follows [Google’s Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) and [image guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images). The articles use server rendering, descriptive titles, canonical URLs, BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, visible review dates, source citations, and local responsive WebP images.

Google indexing, rich-result eligibility and rankings require a publicly accessible deployment and remain Google’s decision. This change does not claim Search Console verification or submission.

## Editorial redesign

The current article model is `data/gameStories.ts`. The pages now focus on project origins, development, process, team and headquarters. FAQs and fictional world introductions were removed. The article uses solid black and charcoal surfaces, opaque text, red / orange / yellow accents, and 10px panel corners aligned with the homepage. The existing site navigation and footer are retained.

The MLBB headquarters image was updated to [MOONTON’s official website photograph](https://en.moonton.com/), locally stored as `moonton-headquarters.webp` and its responsive 640px version. Copyright remains with MOONTON Games; the source credit is shown with the image.

## Gradient editorial refresh

The latest local design replaces the plain black reading column with a red-to-pink cover, alternating opaque plum and berry gradient sections, wider editorial text layouts, full-frame developer photography, and a split map panel. SiteNav and SiteFooter remain the shared components. The contents panel, key-date summary, headquarters banner, and closing promotion remain removed.
