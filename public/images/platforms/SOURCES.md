# Platform marks

Official artwork, displayed through CSS masks with KeroSpace's red, pink and purple gradient. The artwork itself keeps its original geometry. All marks use the same 28 × 28 px display box; the small captions distinguish Mac from iOS.

- Windows: https://learn.microsoft.com/media/logos/logo_windows.svg
- Apple: Apple navigation logo from https://www.apple.com/ (viewBox tightened to the mark).
- Android: `android-bot-head` symbol from https://www.android.com/ (viewBox tightened to the mark).
- PlayStation: `ps-icon_brand_playstation-family` from the official PlayStation website's `ps-icons-map__dig__brand.svg` sprite. ViewBox tightened to the mark.
- Xbox: original transparent PNG embedded in the official Xbox site's favicon asset, https://cms-assets.xboxservices.com/assets/1f/f1/1ff14720-aa8b-4642-8b4a-10096d5cf03b.ico?n=favicon.ico .
